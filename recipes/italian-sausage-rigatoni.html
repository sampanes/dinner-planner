Italian Sausage Rigatoni

Italian Sausage Rigatoni: 7 ingredients, 3 steps. Ratings: pink 3/5, blue 3/5.

Ingredients

  • 12 tablespoonromesco sauce
  • 6 tablespoonparmesan cheese
  • 8 ouncepeas
  • 4 unitstock concentrate
  • 14 ouncerigatoni pasta
  • 18 ounceitalian pork sausage
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. Remove sausage casing if needed. In oven tray, combine sausage (broken up), rigatoni, peas, romesco, stock concentrate, and 1 1/4 cups hot water. Stir to mix.

  2. Cover tray tightly with foil and bake 20 min. Uncover, stir gently, and bake another 12-15 min until sausage is cooked and pasta is al dente.

  3. Top with Parmesan and serve.
