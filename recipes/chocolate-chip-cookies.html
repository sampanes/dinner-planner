Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate Chip Cookies: 7 ingredients, 5 steps. Ratings: pink 1/5, blue 1/5.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cupsflour
  • 1 cupbutter
  • 3/4 cupbrown sugar
  • 3/4 cupsugar
  • 2 largeeggs
  • 2 cupschocolate chips
  • 1 tspvanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).

  2. Cream butter and sugars until fluffy.

  3. Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla.

  4. Mix in flour, then stir in chocolate chips.

  5. Drop spoonfuls onto baking sheets and bake for 9-11 minutes.
