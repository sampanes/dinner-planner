Garlic Butter Shrimp Scampi

Garlic Butter Shrimp Scampi: 12 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 4/5, blue 3/5.

Ingredients

  • 1 unitstock concentrate
  • 1 unitlemon
  • 10 ounceshrimp
  • 2 clovegarlic
  • 6 ouncelinguine pasta
  • 3 tablespoonparmesan cheese
  • 1 unitbroccoli
  • 1 teaspoonchili flakes
  • 1 tablespoonoil
  • 3 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. Cut broccoli into florets. Zest and quarter lemon. Mince garlic.

  2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 12-15 minutes until crispy.

  3. Cook linguine in salted boiling water 9-11 minutes until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.

  4. Soften butter and mix with lemon zest, half the Parmesan, a pinch of garlic, and chili flakes.

  5. Pat shrimp dry. Toss with olive oil, remaining garlic, salt, and pepper. Cook in a hot pan 2-4 minutes until opaque.

  6. Add pasta, broccoli, stock concentrate, garlic butter, and reserved pasta water to shrimp. Toss until coated. Finish with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Serve topped with remaining Parmesan and chili flakes.
