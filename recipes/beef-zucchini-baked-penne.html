Beef & Zucchini Baked Penne

Beef & Zucchini Baked Penne: 14 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 5/5, blue 3/5.

Ingredients

  • 2 unitzucchini
  • 24 ouncepenne pasta
  • 40 ounceground beef
  • 4 tablespoonitalian seasoning
  • 4 teaspoongarlic powder
  • 2 unittomato paste
  • 6 unitstock concentrate
  • 16 ouncericotta cheese
  • 1 cupmozzarella cheese
  • 2 teaspoonchili flakes
  • 28 ouncemarinara sauce
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper
  • 4 teaspoonoil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Boil water. Quarter zucchini lengthwise, slice into quarter-moons.

  2. Cook penne until al dente, 9-11 minutes. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, drain, and set aside.

  3. In a large pan, heat oil. Add beef, zucchini, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook until beef is done and zucchini is tender, 5-7 minutes. Drain grease if needed.

  4. In the pasta pot, combine beef mixture, marinara, tomato paste, stock concentrate, and 1/2 cup pasta water. Boil 2-3 min until thickened. Stir in penne. Season to taste.

  5. Transfer to a greased 9x13 dish. Dollop with ricotta, sprinkle with mozzarella. Bake 10-12 minutes until melted, then broil 2-3 minutes until golden.

  6. Let cool 5 minutes. Top with chili flakes and serve.
