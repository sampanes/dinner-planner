Pork Sausage Rigatoni Rosa

Pork Sausage Rigatoni Rosa: 14 ingredients, 4 steps. Ratings: pink 3/5, blue 4/5.

Ingredients

  • 6 ouncerigatoni pasta
  • 1 unitzucchini
  • 1 clovegarlic
  • 9 ounceitalian pork sausage
  • 1 unittomato paste
  • 4 tablespooncream cheese
  • 1 unitstock concentrate
  • 1 teaspoonchili flakes
  • 6 tablespoonparmesan cheese
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper
  • 2 teaspoonoil
  • 1/2 teaspoonsugar
  • 1 tablespoonbutter

Steps

  1. Boil salted water and cook rigatoni 9-11 min until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain. Slice zucchini into half-moons and mince garlic.

  2. Saute zucchini in oil 5-6 min. Remove. Cook sausage in same pan until almost done.

  3. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1-2 min. Stir in cream cheese, stock, 1/2 cup pasta water, butter, sugar, and chili flakes. Mix until smooth.

  4. Add pasta and zucchini. Stir in half the Parmesan. Add more water as needed for creamy texture. Top with remaining Parmesan and serve.
