Cavatappi Beef Ragu

Cavatappi Beef Ragu: 12 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 4/5, blue 3/5.

Ingredients

  • 6 ouncecarrot
  • 1/4 ounceparsley
  • 12 ouncecavatappi pasta
  • 20 ounceground beef
  • 2 tablespoonitalian seasoning
  • 28 ouncemarinara sauce
  • 4 tablespooncream cheese
  • 1/2 cupparmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoonoil
  • 2 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Boil a pot of salted water. Peel and finely chop carrot. Roughly chop parsley leaves.

  2. Add cavatappi and cook until al dente, 9-11 min. Drain and set aside.

  3. In a pan, heat oil and cook carrot until browned and tender, 6-8 min. Season with salt and pepper.

  4. Push carrot to the side. Add more oil, then beef. Brown and break up beef, season with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Combine with carrot.

  5. Add marinara and 1/3 cup water. Simmer 3-4 min. Stir in cream cheese. Add pasta, butter, and half the parsley. Stir to coat. Adjust seasoning.

  6. Serve in bowls topped with Parmesan and remaining parsley.
