Zucchini & Tomato Calzones

Steps

  1. Adjust rack to middle position (middle and top positions for 4 servings) and preheat oven to 425 degrees.

    Sprinkle flour over a clean work surface (reserve a little for dusting your hands in both this step and Step 4). Remove pizza dough from packaging and place on floured surface.

    Shape each piece of dough into a ball. TIP: Dust your hands with flour to prevent sticking.

    Cover with a clean kitchen towel and let rest at room temperature for 20 minutes.

  2. While dough rests, wash and dry produce.

    Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; thinly slice crosswise into half-moons. Halve tomatoes. Peel and mince or grate garlic. Zest and quarter lemon (for 4 servings, zest one lemon and quarter both).

  3. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.

    Add zucchini and tomatoes and cook, stirring, until zucchini is lightly browned and softened and tomatoes are broken down, 5-6 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.

    Turn off heat; stir in garlic.

    In a small bowl, combine ricotta, half the lemon zest, basil paste, and lemon juice to taste. Season with salt and pepper.

  4. Once dough has rested 20 minutes, line a baking sheet (two sheets for 4 servings) with parchment paper.

    Carefully press and stretch each dough ball into a rough oval shape and place on one side of prepared sheet (you'll have two dough ovals per baking sheet).

    TIP: If needed, use a rolling pin to help roll out dough.

    Cover with a clean kitchen towel and let rest 15 minutes more (this will make stretching the dough into a larger shape even easier!).

  5. Once dough has rested 15 minutes, use floured hands to press and stretch dough on baking sheet into larger circle shapes, 8-10 inches wide.

    Evenly spread lemon-basil ricotta over half of each piece of dough. Top with zucchini mixture.

    Fold empty half of dough over filling to form calzones; crimp edges to seal. Brush each calzone with 1 tsp oil, then use a knife to make a small slit in the top of each.

    Bake on middle rack until golden brown and crisp, 18-22 minutes (for 4 servings, bake on middle and top racks, swapping rack positions halfway through).

  6. Let calzones cool slightly, 3-4 minutes, then divide between plates. Serve with marinara on the side for dipping.
