Beef Bulgogi Meatballs

Beef Bulgogi Meatballs: 13 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 4/5, blue 1/5.

Ingredients

  • 24 ouncecarrot
  • 4 unitscallions
  • 2 thumbginger
  • 1.5 cupjasmine rice
  • 20 ounceground beef
  • 1/2 cuppanko breadcrumbs
  • 16 tablespoonbulgogi sauce
  • 4 tablespoonsour cream
  • 1 teaspoonsriracha
  • 1 tablespoonsesame seeds
  • 2 teaspoonoil
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Peel and slice carrots; roast on baking sheet with oil, salt, and pepper until tender, 20-25 minutes.

  2. Peel and mince ginger. Slice scallions, separating whites and greens.

  3. Saute half the ginger in oil; add rice, water, and salt. Simmer until tender, 15-18 minutes.

  4. Mix beef, scallion whites, panko, remaining ginger, and some bulgogi sauce. Form into meatballs and bake 14-16 min.

  5. Mix sour cream with sriracha and water until drizzle consistency. Toss cooked meatballs in remaining bulgogi sauce.

  6. Plate rice with carrots and meatballs. Drizzle crema on top and garnish with scallion greens and sesame seeds.
