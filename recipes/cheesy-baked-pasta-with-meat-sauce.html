Cheesy Baked Pasta with Meat Sauce

Cheesy Baked Pasta with Meat Sauce: 8 ingredients, 4 steps. Ratings: pink 5/5, blue 5/5.

Ingredients

  • 12 ozpenne pasta
  • 7.5 ozmozzarella cheese
  • 20 ozground beef
  • 16 ozmarinara sauce
  • 2 tsp, gratedgarlic
  • 1 tbspoil
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add penne and cook until al dente, about 8 minutes. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain and set aside.

  2. Preheat broiler to high. Grate garlic and tear mozzarella into 1/2-inch pieces. In a large ovenproof skillet, heat olive oil over medium-high. Add beef, salt, and pepper; cook until browned and no longer pink, 3-5 minutes.

  3. Stir garlic into beef; cook 1 minute. Add marinara and reserved pasta water. Simmer briefly, then stir in cooked pasta. Season to taste.

  4. Top with mozzarella. Broil until cheese is melted and bubbling, 2-3 minutes. Serve hot.
