Pub-Style Shepherd's Pie

Pub-Style Shepherd's Pie: 16 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 4/5, blue 5/5.

Ingredients

  • 32 ouncepotatoes
  • 5 ouncecelery
  • 1/4 ouncethyme
  • 4 tablespoonsour cream
  • 13.76 ouncecrushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoonflour
  • 1 cupcheddar cheese
  • 6 ouncecarrot

  • 20 ounceground beef
  • 4 unitstock concentrate
  • 1 tablespoonoil
  • 3 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Heat broiler to high. Dice potatoes into 1/2-inch pieces. Prep thyme, carrot, celery, and onion.

  2. Boil potatoes in salted water until tender, 15-20 minutes. Drain, return to pot, and mash with sour cream, 1 tsp thyme, and 2 TBSP butter. Season with salt and pepper. Cover and set aside.

  3. Heat oil in ovenproof pan over medium-high. Add carrot; cook 2-3 minutes. Add celery, onion, and more oil; cook until tender, 5-7 minutes. Stir in garlic powder and 2 tsp thyme; cook 30 seconds.

  4. Add ground beef; season and cook until browned, 4-6 minutes. Stir in flour; cook 1 minute.

  5. Add crushed tomatoes and 1/2 cup water. Stir in stock concentrates. Bring to a boil and cook until thickened, 1-2 minutes. Season to taste. (Transfer to baking dish if pan is not ovenproof.)

  6. Spread mashed potatoes on top, leaving a gap around the edges. Sprinkle cheddar over top. Broil 3-4 minutes until browned. Serve hot.
