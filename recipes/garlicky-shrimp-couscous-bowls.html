Garlicky Shrimp Couscous Bowls

Garlicky Shrimp Couscous Bowls: 13 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 5/5, blue 2/5.

Ingredients

  • 8 ouncebroccoli
  • 2 clovegarlic
  • 1/4 ounceparsley
  • 1 unitlemon
  • 1 teaspoonchili flakes
  • 2.5 ounceisraeli couscous
  • 1 unitveggie stock concentrate
  • 10 ounceshrimp
  • 1 teaspoongarlic powder
  • 5 teaspoonoil
  • 1 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. Cut broccoli if needed. Mince garlic, chop parsley, quarter lemon.

  2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, chili flakes, salt, and pepper. Roast 12-15 minutes.

  3. In a small pot, saute half the garlic in olive oil. Add couscous, stock concentrate, water, and salt. Simmer covered 6-8 minutes until tender.

  4. Pat shrimp dry. Cook in olive oil with garlic powder, salt, and pepper 3-4 minutes. Add water and cook until evaporated. Lower heat; add butter, remaining garlic, chili flakes. Toss with half the parsley and lemon juice.

  5. Fluff couscous. Stir in remaining parsley, lemon juice, and olive oil. Season.

  6. Divide couscous into bowls. Top with shrimp and broccoli. Spoon garlic butter over and serve with lemon wedges.
