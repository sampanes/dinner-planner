Mushroom Ravioli in a Tomato Cream Sauce

Mushroom Ravioli in a Tomato Cream Sauce: 11 ingredients, 5 steps. Ratings: pink 4/5, blue 4/5.

Ingredients

  • 4 ouncegrape tomatoes
  • 4 ouncericotta cheese
  • 1 tablespoonitalian seasoning
  • 14 ouncemarinara sauce
  • 1 unitstock concentrate
  • 2 tablespooncream cheese
  • 9 ouncemushroom ravioli
  • 2 teaspoonoil
  • 1 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper

Steps

  1. Bring a medium pot of salted water to a boil. Halve large tomatoes. Stir ricotta with olive oil, salt, and pepper.

  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan. Add tomatoes and Italian seasoning; cook 2-3 minutes. Stir in stock concentrate, marinara, cream cheese, and water. Simmer until thickened.

  3. Cook ravioli in boiling water until tender and floating, 3-4 minutes. Reserve 1/4 cup pasta water, then drain.

  4. Add ravioli and butter to sauce; toss to coat. Add reserved pasta water as needed for creaminess. Season.

  5. Divide between bowls and top with a dollop of ricotta.
