Sweet Chili Beef & Green Bean Bowls

Sweet Chili Beef & Green Bean Bowls: 13 ingredients, 6 steps. Ratings: pink 2/5, blue 1/5.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cupjasmine rice
  • 12 ouncegreen beans
  • 1 unitlime
  • 1/4 ouncecilantro
  • 20 ounceground beef
  • 8 tablespoonsweet soy glaze
  • 2 ouncesweet thai chili sauce
  • 2 unitcrispy fried onions
  • 1 teaspoonoil
  • 4 tablespoonbutter
  • to tastesalt
  • to tastepepper
  • 1 teaspoonsugar

Steps

  1. Cook rice in salted water until tender, 15-18 min. Set aside.

  2. Trim and chop green beans, quarter lime, chop cilantro.

  3. Brown ground beef in oil, 3-4 min undisturbed, then break up and cook through. Drain excess grease if needed.

  4. Steam green beans in microwave with splash of water (1-2 min) or on stovetop (5-7 min).

  5. Add green beans to beef along with chili sauce, 2 1/2 TBSP sweet soy glaze, 1/2 tsp sugar, and 1 TBSP butter. Cook 1-2 min. Finish with lime juice.

  6. Fluff rice, stir in 1 TBSP butter, season. Serve rice topped with beef, onions, cilantro, and lime wedges.
